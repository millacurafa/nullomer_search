A little context

Small words.
Big possibilities.

DNA is written with four letters. The order—and absence—of those letters can tell us something about the genomes we study.

01

What’s a DNA word?

A k-mer is just a stretch of DNA that is k letters long. “AATG” is a 4-mer.

02

What’s a nullomer?

A nullomer is a DNA word that does not appear in a particular genome. This tool finds the shortest absent length up to your chosen limit.

03

What does “shared” mean here?

We look for the longest exact, continuous DNA sequence present in every genome you selected. It’s specific to those input files—not every organism on Earth.

✳

Science note This project is inspired by Hampikian & Andersen’s research on absent DNA sequences. Absence alone cannot tell us why a sequence is missing or whether it has a biological effect.
